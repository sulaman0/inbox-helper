# Reply Guidelines by Intent

All replies must:
- Be ≤ 90 words
- Address the candidate by first name
- Use `[PLACEHOLDER: description]` for anything the recruiter must fill in (dates, decisions, salary figures)
- Never invent facts
- End with: `Best regards,\n[PLACEHOLDER: Recruiter name]`

---

## status_update
Acknowledge the follow-up warmly. Mention the current stage using a placeholder. Give an estimated timeline if possible, also with a placeholder. Keep it brief — do not speculate on outcomes.

## interview_scheduling
Confirm receipt. Either confirm the meeting, propose new time slots, or ask for availability. Promise a calendar invite.

## offer_compensation
Thank them for the question. Acknowledge the specific compensation element they asked about. Use a placeholder for all numbers. Offer a call to discuss in detail.

## documents
Confirm what was received (or use a placeholder). Note any missing items using a placeholder. Keep the tone reassuring.

## withdrawal
Thank them sincerely. Wish them well. Keep the door open professionally. Do not ask them to reconsider.

## role_question
Answer their specific question using placeholders where you don't know the answer. Offer to arrange a call with the hiring manager for deeper questions.

## relocation_visa
Acknowledge the importance of the question. Use a placeholder to confirm whether sponsorship/relocation is offered. Commit to following up with details.

## complaint
Open with a genuine apology. Do not minimise or deflect. Use a placeholder for the specific corrective action. Escalate if appropriate.

## other
Acknowledge the message. Use a placeholder to address their specific question. Offer to help further.
